CMTacTool
=========

Usage
-----

Open a text file containing your benchmark results in the following format:

tactic_name scored-conceded points games wins-draws-losses

Spaces are used to separate the fields. As an example, the following line

FuryOfTheBeast            53-38 44 26 12-8-6

means that the tactic FuryOfTheBeast scored 53 goals, conceded 38 and scored 44 points. This
in 26 games, by winning 12 games, drawing 8 and losing 6.

The program cannot handle tactic names with spaces in it, so replace spaces with underlines.


Introduction
------------

Use the menu to load the attached iraklis_sample.txt file.

You will see several columns of data: tactic name, goals, conceded, points, pts_avg and #.

Tactic name is the name of the tactic, all benchmark runs with the same tactic name are
assumed to belong to the same tactic.

The goals column is of the form: 

avg+-interval 

the avg is the average goals scored by the tactic in the benchmarks present (the sample mean).
The interval is the 95% confidence interval, that is where the true average is with 95% 
probability.

As an example, a goal column of 54.81+-1.02 means that the tactic scored on average 54.81
goals in the benchmarks so far, and its true average is w. 95% chance between 53.79--55.83

The conceded column is the same, but for conceded.

The points column is pretty much the same, but instead the 95% confidence interval is presented.

Pts avg is the sample mean of the points.

The # indicates the number of benchmarks available of a particular tactic.


Compare tool
------------

Is able to show you the difference in player positions between two selected tactics. Both tactics 
must be in your tactics folder.

Stats tool
----------

Shows more statistics about a tactic. The most important thing here is the std_dev for points.
That indicates how consistent the tactic is, the lower the value, the more stable it is.
